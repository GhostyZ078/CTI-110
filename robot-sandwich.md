
# Robot Sandwich

## Assumption 
- the robot is in the kitchen
- the bread is on the plate
- the meat is on the plate
- a cup of orange juice already pouredm in a cup

## Numbered Steps
1. split the bread in half
2. place the meat on 1 half
3. place the left over bread ontop of the other bread
4. pick up your orange juice carton
5. pour the orange juice into your cup
6. eat the sandwich
7. drink your orange juice

## Done When
- your sandwich is eaten
- your cup of orange juice is drunk
